Project: NiHarts – Handmade Arts & Crafts E-Commerce Platform

About:

NiHarts is a full-stack e-commerce platform developed for a handmade arts and crafts business, enabling customers to discover and purchase unique handcrafted products through a responsive online storefront.

Description:

  • Frontend: React.js for responsive and interactive user interfaces.
  • Backend: Node.js and Express.js for REST API development.
  • Database: MongoDB for managing products, customers, and orders.
  • Authentication: JWT-based authentication and role-based access control.
  • Security: Bcrypt password hashing and secure account management.
  • Password Recovery: Six-digit OTP verification through Nodemailer.
  • Payment Integration: Razorpay for checkout and online payments.
  • Admin Dashboard: Product, category, customer, and order management.
  • Email Notifications: Automated transactional email communication.
